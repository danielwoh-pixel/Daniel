Talent Analytics · September 2026

Leading Brand Ecosystems

A total of 107 candidates have adidas experience, representing 27.9% of the database and about 2.9 times the Nike count. The Top 10 brands cover 242 unique candidates, or 63.2%. Nike (37), Puma (29) and The North Face (29) form the next tier. Anta, FILA and Li-Ning each account for 10 candidates. Another 141 candidates come primarily from luxury, fashion, specialist outdoor and regional brands.

Talent Analytics · September 2026

Global Nationality Distribution

The database contains 123 US candidates (32.1%). Korean and Chinese candidates account for 51 and 49 respectively. Of the 14 September additions, six are Italian, four Korean and two American, with one British and one Chinese candidate. The ten leading nationalities account for 358 candidates, or 93.5%. The remaining 25 candidates span 15 nationality backgrounds.

Talent Analytics · September 2026

Preferred Work Destinations

China was selected by 156 candidates (40.7%), up by nine from the August snapshot. Japan received 121 selections (31.6%). After excluding candidates already based in each market, China and Japan record 105 and 103 cross-border selections respectively. The US, Korea and Europe received 79, 44 and 38 geographic selections.

Talent Analytics · September 2026

Cross-border Talent Mobility

Of 248 cross-border destination selections, China received 105 and Japan 103 — together 83.9%. US→Japan ranks first with 39 selections, followed by US→China with 28. Among 375 eligible candidates, 193 (51.5%) have at least one cross-border country preference. The top ten routes account for 178 selections, or 71.8% of all flows.

Talent Analytics · September 2026

Salary Distribution

Based on 114 valid salary records from 383 candidates (29.8%). Across 37 valid US records, the median is approximately USD 221K, above China at USD 103K, Germany at USD 92K and Korea at USD 67K. Italy and the UK show higher medians with a strong sample-composition effect. The chart displays 105 records across eight countries. Findings should be interpreted as directional rather than precise market pricing.

Market Watch · September 2026

Converting Luxury Experience for China Teams

Role transferability for premium-brand design talent.

Among the 14 candidates added in September, 10 have Luxury experience and nine selected China. This reflects the output of this month’s targeted talent mapping, not the international market as a whole.

Hiring teams should assess whether candidates can translate premium-brand experience into products suited to the target consumer, price point, development pace and supply ecosystem — across consumer positioning, development feasibility, brand translation and organisational fit.

Hiring Intelligence · September 2026

Hiring Recommendations

A short preview of this month’s priorities. The full recommendation set is shared with the report.

01

Expand the talent pool beyond the Top 10

The Top 10 brands cover 242 candidates, while another 141 come from Luxury, Fashion, specialist outdoor and regional brands.

02

Use different source markets for China and Japan

China can prioritise the US, Korea, Germany, Italy and the UK. Japan can prioritise the US, Germany and China.

03

Create a transferability assessment for Luxury talent

Add a real product task beyond the portfolio to test choices for the target consumer, price point, materials, function and development pace.

04

Compare compensation only on like-for-like role scope

The 114 compensation records cover 29.8% of the database, and the eight markets have different seniority structures.

05

Separate new additions from field maintenance

September added 14 candidates, but compensation, mobility and brand metrics also reflect updates to existing records.
