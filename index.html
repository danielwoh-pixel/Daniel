Leading Brand Ecosystems · September 2026

242 unique candidates (63.2%) have experience at one or more Top 10 brands. adidas remains the largest talent ecosystem with 107 candidates (27.9%), ahead of Nike (37), Puma (29) and The North Face (29).

Global Nationality Distribution · September 2026

American designers lead at 123 (32.1%), followed by Korean talent at 51 and Chinese talent at 49. The Top 10 nationalities represent 93.5% of the pool (358 of 383).

Preferred Work Destinations · September 2026

China received 156 destination selections (40.7%) and Japan 121 (31.6%), extending a dual-center structure for design talent mobility in Asia.

Cross-border Talent Mobility · September 2026

Of 248 cross-border destination selections, China received 105 and Japan 103. US→Japan (39) and US→China (28) lead. 193 eligible candidates (51.5%) have at least one cross-border country preference.

Salary Distribution · September 2026

Based on 114 valid salary records (29.8%). The US leads among larger samples with a median of approximately USD 221K; China, Germany and Korea sit lower, and smaller samples should be read as directional.

Executive Summary

The September database covers 383 global footwear and apparel design professionals. Fourteen candidates were added this month (+3.8%). Additions are concentrated in apparel design and Luxury, further expanding the cross-border talent pool open to China.

  • 242 candidates (63.2%) have Top 10 brand experience; adidas leads with 107.
  • China (156) and Japan (121) lead preferred destinations; 193 eligible candidates (51.5%) show cross-border preference.
  • Salary analysis is based on 114 valid records (29.8%) and should be treated as directional.

Full monthly reports are shared upon request with hiring managers, HR leaders and selected industry partners due to candidate confidentiality.
