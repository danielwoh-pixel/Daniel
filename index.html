October 2026 — Design Talent Intelligence

Data as of 27 October 2026

Month-to-date snapshot

As of 27 October 2026, the database covers 394 footwear and apparel design professionals open to new opportunities — 11 more than the September snapshot, or 2.9%. Additions are more evenly split between apparel and footwear. Outdoor and Luxury are the joint leading categories in that cohort, and flexible engagement preferences have increased.

Interactive Charts

October market analytics.

Five views from the 27 October snapshot. The briefing continues on Insights.

Leading Brand Ecosystems · October 2026

245 unique candidates (62.2%) have experience at one or more Top 10 brands. adidas remains the largest talent ecosystem with 107 candidates (27.2%), ahead of Nike (37), The North Face (31) and PUMA (30).

Global Nationality Distribution · October 2026

American designers lead at 126 (32.0%), followed by Korean talent at 51 and Chinese talent at 49. The Top 10 nationalities represent 93.7% of the pool (369 of 394).

Preferred Work Destinations · October 2026

China received 162 destination selections (41.1%) and Japan 122 (31.0%). Remote, Freelance and Hybrid — 72 selections from 71 candidates — are excluded from this geographic view.

Cross-border Talent Mobility · October 2026

Of 255 cross-border selections, China received 110 and Japan 105. US to Japan (39) and US to China (29) lead. 198 of 386 eligible candidates (51.3%) will consider a cross-border move.

Salary Distribution · October 2026

Based on 116 valid salary records (29.4%). The US median is about USD 221K across 37 records. China, Germany and Korea sit lower. Japan and France have fewer than five records and are directional only. The chart shows 107 records across eight countries.
