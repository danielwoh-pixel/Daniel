Leading Brand Ecosystems · August 2026

244 unique candidates (66.1%) have experience at one or more leading brands. adidas remains the largest talent ecosystem with 106 candidates (28.7%), ahead of Nike (36) and Puma (29).

Global Nationality Distribution · August 2026

American designers lead at 121 (32.8%), followed by Chinese and Korean talent at 47 each. The Top 10 nationalities represent 93.0% of the pool (343 of 369).

Preferred Work Destinations · August 2026

China received 147 destination selections (39.8%) and Japan 120 (32.5%), forming a dual-center structure for design talent mobility in Asia.

Cross-border Talent Mobility · August 2026

Of 237 cross-border destination selections, Japan received 101 and China 95. US→Japan (38) and US→China (25) lead. 183 eligible candidates (51.0%) have at least one cross-border country preference.

Salary Distribution · August 2026

Based on 107 valid salary records (29.0%). The US leads among larger samples with a median of approximately USD 220K; China, Germany and Korea offer more competitive salary structures.
